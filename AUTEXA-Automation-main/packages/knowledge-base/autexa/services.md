# Autexa AI Automation Services

Autexa specializes in building intelligent AI agents that automate customer communication and lead management. We deploy agents across multiple channels to help businesses qualify leads, answer FAQs, and streamline operations.

## Available Channels

### 1. Website Chat Widget
- **Description**: A custom, responsive chat widget embedded directly on your website to engage visitors 24/7.
- **Features**: Real-time response, automatic lead capture, pipeline synchronization.
- **Estimated Setup Cost**: $500 - $1,500 setup fee (rough estimate depending on customization).

### 2. WhatsApp Business Integration
- **Description**: Connect our AI agent directly to your official WhatsApp Business API.
- **Features**: Highly conversational, supports automated follow-ups, handles rich media and quick replies.
- **Estimated Setup Cost**: $800 - $2,000 setup fee (rough estimate, excludes Meta API charges).

### 3. Instagram Direct Message (DM) Automation
- **Description**: Automated response system to handle incoming DMs, story mentions, and comments.
- **Features**: Drive engagement directly to sales funnels, handle service inquiries, qualify buyers.
- **Estimated Setup Cost**: $600 - $1,800 setup fee (rough estimate).

## Core Capabilities
- **Lead Qualification**: Automatically determine caller intent (buying, browsing, support) and priority (hot, warm, cold).
- **CRM Integration**: Automatically push qualified leads to Hubspot, Salesforce, or custom internal systems.
- **Knowledge Base RAG**: Connects custom business documents (pricing, FAQs) so the AI always answers accurately.

*Note: All prices listed are rough estimates for deployment and setup. Custom integration requirements will influence final scoping and costs.*
