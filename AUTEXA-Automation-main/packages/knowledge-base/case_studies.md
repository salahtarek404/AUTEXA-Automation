# Autexa Case Studies

Read about how our clients have transformed their operations and increased conversion rates using Autexa AI Automation.

## Case Study 1: Apex Fitness Group (WhatsApp Lead Qualification)
- **Client**: Mid-sized fitness club franchise with 5 locations.
- **Challenge**: The sales team spent hours qualifying cold leads from Instagram ads, leading to delayed response times and high drop-offs.
- **Solution**: Deployed an Autexa WhatsApp Business Agent connected to their ad campaign. The agent engaged users instantly, qualified their intent/budget, and automatically booked warm leads into CRM.
- **Results**:
  - Response time dropped from 3 hours to under 5 seconds.
  - Lead conversion rate increased by 22% in the first 30 days.
  - Over 60% of leads qualified automatically.

## Case Study 2: Nova E-Commerce (24/7 Support and FAQ Agent)
- **Client**: Fast-growing apparel brand handling 500+ customer inquiries daily.
- **Challenge**: Customer support tickets piled up over the weekend, leading to unhappy buyers and lost sales.
- **Solution**: Installed the Autexa Web Chat Widget loaded with their FAQ, shipping policies, and product details.
- **Results**:
  - Handled 75% of customer support queries autonomously without human intervention.
  - Weekend checkout abandonment reduced by 15%.
  - CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score) rose to 4.8/5.0.

## Case Study 3: Vanguard Real Estate (High-Priority Lead Routing)
- **Client**: Luxury property brokerage.
- **Challenge**: Missing inquiries on multi-million dollar listings outside of office hours.
- **Solution**: Setup a multi-channel (Instagram + Web) agent programmed to escalate high-budget buying leads to on-call brokers via SMS/email alerts.
- **Results**:
  - 100% of high-intent inquiries captured instantly.
  - Secured 3 major luxury property contracts within two months from off-hours leads.
