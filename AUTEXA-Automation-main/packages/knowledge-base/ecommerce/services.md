# Global E-commerce Shop AI Assistant

Our AI assistant helps customers track packages, handle returns, and recommend products.

## Support Scope
- **Order Tracking**: Enter order ID to fetch shipping status and carrier link.
- **Product Recommendation**: Dynamic matching based on size, style, or color preferences.
- **Returns & Exchanges**: Initiates exchange labels for unworn items.

## Shipping & Return Fees
- **Standard Shipping**: Free for orders over $50, otherwise $4.99 flat rate.
- **Express Shipping (2-day)**: $12.99 flat rate.
- **Returns processing**: Free within 30 days of delivery. Excludes clearance items.
