# Autexa AI Automation FAQ

Here are the most common questions regarding the setup, performance, and security of Autexa's AI agents.

### Q: How long does setup take?
A: A standard website widget or Instagram automation setup typically takes 1 to 2 weeks. Custom enterprise integrations or complex WhatsApp routing may take 4 to 6 weeks.

### Q: Can the AI agent book meetings directly?
A: Yes! The agent can call tools to check availability and schedule meetings directly into Calendly or Google Calendar. (Note: Booking/scheduling is part of our upcoming Phase 3 feature set).

### Q: How accurate is the AI in answering customer questions?
A: Our RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline ensures that the AI answers using only the uploaded business documents. If the AI cannot find the answer in the knowledge base, it will politely request contact info to escalate to a human representative.

### Q: Is my business data secure?
A: Absolutely. We follow strict data-handling policies. Conversations are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not use your proprietary business data to train public LLM foundation models.

### Q: Does the agent support multiple languages?
A: Yes, our agents dynamically detect the customer's language (supporting over 50 languages including English, Spanish, Arabic, French, and German) and reply in the same language.
