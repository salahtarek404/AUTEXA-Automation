# Premier Real Estate Agent Assistant

Our AI assistant helps you browse property listings, schedule showings, and connect with lead brokers.

## Our Services
- **Showing Scheduling**: Coordinate tours for residential and commercial listings.
- **Listing Analysis**: Retrieve square footage, neighborhood metrics, school districts.
- **Valuation Estimation**: Request a complimentary Comparative Market Analysis (CMA).

## Pricing & Commissions
- **Buyer Representation**: Generally free (paid by selling broker commission split).
- **Listing Commission**: 5% to 6% of final closing price (split 50/50 with buyer's agent).
- **Tour Booking Fee**: $0 (absolutely free showings for pre-approved buyers).
