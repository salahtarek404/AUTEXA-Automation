# Gourmet Restaurant AI Assistant Services

We offer automated reservation booking, order placement, menu FAQs, and party planning assistance.

## Available Menu & Offerings
- **Table Booking**: Reserve private tables, standard tables, or patio spots.
- **Specials**: Weekend Chef's tasting menu, local wines.
- **Event Scopes**: Private rooms for up to 50 guests.

## Reservation Rates & Pricing
- **Standard Reservation**: Free (requires credit card hold for parties > 6).
- **Chef's Table Experience**: $120 per person (includes 7-course pairing).
- **Private Room Hire**: $500 minimum spend on food and beverage.
- **Cancellation Policy**: Cancel up to 24 hours in advance without fee, otherwise $25 per head fee.
